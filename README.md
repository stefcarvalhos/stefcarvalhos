🎓 **Education:** Bachelor's Degree in Law - Universidade Cândido Mendes  
💼 **Previous Experience:** Senior Legal Analyst at Ambev S.A.  
🔧 **Tech Skills:** Power BI, Power Automate, Microsoft Forms, Process Automation  
🚀 **Career Transition:** Leveraging legal analytical thinking to solve complex programming challenges

### 🔄 My Journey

After years working as a legal professional, developing critical analysis skills, complex problem-solving abilities, and project management expertise, I decided to redirect my career toward technology. My experience at Ambev on transformational projects using automation tools (Power BI, Power Automate) sparked my passion for tech and coding.

Today, I combine my legal strategic thinking background with software development, creating efficient and innovative technological solutions. The transition from legal logic to computational logic has been challenging and incredibly rewarding!
